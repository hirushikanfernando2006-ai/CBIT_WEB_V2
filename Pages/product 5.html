EQQUALBERRY SWIMMING POOL TONER PAD

Price: Rs. 5,400

PRODUCT DEFINITION

Dual-textured daily toner pads soaked in exfoliating and hydrating essence to remove dead skin cells and refine rough skin texture for a clear, glass-skin finish.

HOW TO USE IT

  1. After cleansing, use the embossed side of the pad to gently wipe across the face, avoiding the eye area.
  2. Flip to the smooth side and wipe across the face to soothe and hydrate skin.
  3. Gently pat the remaining essence into skin until fully absorbed.
